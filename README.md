# Nagoya Hill Condominium · 3D residences

Interactive 3D models of the Studio and 1 Bedroom residences of Nagoya Hill Condominium, Tower 2 (Batam):
Studio 06A, 10A, 12A and 16A, and 1 Bedroom 03B, 05C, 06D, 08D and 10D.

Open the site, choose a residence under **Discover Residences**, and it opens in the next panel. Rotate the cut-away model,
double-tap the floor to step inside, and tap doors, cabinets, drawers, taps, the shower, lamps, the TV and the ACs.
Day, dusk and night lighting and three finish palettes can be switched in the viewer.

Built with three.js. Interior design concept for illustration only; sizes, materials and colours may change, and nothing
here forms part of an offer or contract.
