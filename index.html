Inside every residence

One interior language across the types: warm walnut, fluted oak and charcoal, bronze perforated metal, travertine-look stone, smoked glass and matte black.

  • Bed wallCharcoal lower panels, a concealed LED grazing a fluted light-oak band, a flat oak upper band with a long recessed niche, a low walnut bed
  • TV wallGreige straight-grain wood either side of a bronze-framed perforated panel, a black slot light, a floating light-greige console
  • Display cabinetA bronze-glass cabinet with lit shelves between charcoal shaker cabinets that open onto folded linen
  • KitchenWalnut base run, travertine-look top and splashback, smoked-glass uppers, two-zone induction, a graphite fridge column
  • BathroomGrey stone walls, a walk-in rain shower behind fixed glass, a lit niche, a floating walnut vanity, a wall-hung WC with jet washer
  • Living + diningIn the one-bedroom types: a bouclé sofa with an arc lamp, a stone-topped dining table for four under a pendant
  • BalconyStone wall, a coffee table with a cordless lamp, an olive tree, a frameless glass balustrade, the outdoor AC in a perforated cage
  • Smart entryA dark walnut entry door with a keypad lock, and a split AC above it
